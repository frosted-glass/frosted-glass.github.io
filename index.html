Questions

What file format do I get?

You receive a .rbxm file.

Can I use the assets in a commercial game?

You are allowed to use these assets in a commercial project, but you aren't allowed to redistribute, sell or pirate any of out assets..

Do I get updates?

There are no updates after a purchase of a specific asset.

What if my bio code is not accepted?

Check that the code is on its own line in your bio, with no extra characters, then try again. Profile changes can take a minute to show.

What payment methods can I use?

We only accept Robux payments.

Do I get future assets by purchasing the bundle?

Yes, you do receive any future assets by purchasing our bundle.

Will I have access to my purchased assets anytime?

Yes, by verifying your identity, you can see all of your purchased assets.

Are the transactions secure?

Yes, all transactions processed by Node are secure.

What is Node?

Node is our transaction system, used to process all payments.

Who owns Node?

We are the owners and creators of Node. If you see another website using Node, it is not us. We do not share our technologies with other entities.
